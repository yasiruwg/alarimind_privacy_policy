Quick summary. Alarimind is designed to provide sleep-related tools. We aim to collect only what is needed to operate and improve the app, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Alarimind mobile application and related services. It does not apply to third-party websites, app stores, operating systems, or services that have their own privacy policies.

Alarimind is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Please consult a qualified professional about health concerns.

2. Information we may handle

The exact information handled depends on the features you use and the configuration of the released app.

  • Information you enter: alarm times, reminder settings, sleep preferences, and other settings you choose to save.
  • Device permissions: notification, alarm, vibration, and audio-related permissions when you enable features that need them.
  • Local app data: settings and preferences may be stored on your device so the app can work. Offline sleep sounds are intended to play without sending their content to us.
  • Technical information: the app or a service provider may receive limited device, diagnostic, or usage information where needed for reliability, security, or support.

Analytics and advertising: Alarimind uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on your device settings, consent choices, and the app’s configuration, Google and its partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and improve advertising. You can manage advertising and privacy choices through your device settings and any consent controls presented in the app. For more information, review Google’s information about how it uses data from apps and sites .

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide alarms, reminders, sleep-cycle calculations, sound playback, notifications, app settings, customer support, security, troubleshooting, and improvements to the app.

We do not use sleep settings or other app data to make medical decisions about you. We do not sell personal information. If the app uses a third-party service, that service may process information under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Storage, retention, and sharing

Data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s data, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove it. Any information handled by a service provider is retained only as long as reasonably needed for its stated purpose or as required by law.

We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer. We do not share information for unrelated third-party marketing without the applicable permission or legal basis.

5. Your choices and rights

You can manage permissions through your device settings, change or delete saved app settings, and uninstall the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request before responding. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

6. Children’s privacy

Alarimind is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

7. Security and changes

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. No app or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We may update this policy when the app, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
